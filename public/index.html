Before you connect

A few useful answers.

Start with the basics, then see the product with your own company context.

What happens after I add my website?+

Smark builds a company foundation from your public site, then runs six connected analyses covering intelligence, SEO, AI visibility, competitors, audience, and content.

Does Smark publish content automatically?+

No. Agents surface opportunities and prepare work, while you keep approval control.

Which AI providers can I use?+

You can connect OpenAI, Anthropic, Google Gemini, or OpenRouter and validate the key during setup.

Can I export the reports?+

Yes. Reports can produce a polished PDF, an editable presentation, and an operational spreadsheet when useful.
